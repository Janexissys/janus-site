Product

Janus DTM OS

Your workflows. Gated, audited, and governed — without replacement.

AI systems are fast, and the future of work. They're also opaque. They drift, hallucinate, and make decisions nobody can trace. When something goes wrong, nobody knows why. Or when it started.

Janus fixes that.

You bring your workflow. We install it, gate it, and run it. When something matters — when an action is risky, irreversible, or outside defined bounds — Janus stops and surfaces it to the operator. The operator decides. Janus remembers, the system sustains.

Every decision is logged. Every override is audited. Every workflow runs the same way every time, until a human says otherwise.

What you get

■

Full workflow visibility

A Kanban that shows you what's running, what's blocked, and why. At a glance.

■

Grounded chat interface

A chat interface grounded in your workflow's history, not a generic AI assistant.

■

Institutional memory

Captures the why behind every decision so your team never makes the same mistake twice.

■

Governance gates

Stops the workflow before bad output reaches anyone downstream.

Built for operations leaders who are done guessing what their AI is doing.
