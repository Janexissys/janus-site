DTM Consulting

Better work starts with understanding the work.

You don't need a fully formed automation project to talk to DTM. You may simply know that a process is taking too much time, depending on too few people, or getting harder to manage. We help make the work understandable before deciding what technology belongs in it.

Understand what's really happening

Find the real problem first.

We work through the process with the people who know it: what starts the work, what decisions are made, where things stall, and where exceptions change the plan.

The goal is to find the real problem, not sell a tool before we've understood it.

Design a workable approach

The useful answer is not always the most complicated one.

We help clarify responsibilities, decision boundaries, practical checks and opportunities for automation. Some work needs a better process. Some benefits from ordinary software. Some calls for AI.

Leave with a way forward

A plan your team can use.

An engagement can focus on a clearer workflow, better requirements, options for a pilot, or a practical plan your team can use. The scope is agreed together before work begins.

Independent of Janus

Consulting does not require adopting Janus.

DTM is building Janus OS, but the work and recommendations should make sense for your organization on their own. If Janus is a fit, we can discuss it separately.

Start with a conversation.

Tell us what you're working through. We can discuss whether there's a useful way for DTM to help.
