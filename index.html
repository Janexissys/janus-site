Janus OS

AI should free your attention, not demand more of it.

Complicated work. Simple management.

Your team already knows how to do the work. They shouldn't have to become AI engineers to delegate it. Janus OS is being built to turn that expertise into repeatable AI-assisted workflows, with clear rules, checks and people retaining authority over decisions that matter.

Meet Arbiter

You shouldn't need to become an AI engineer to explain a job.

Arbiter is Janus's first application: a guide designed to draw out how people actually do their work, what exceptions matter and which decisions belong to them.

The employee remains the expert. Arbiter helps shape that expertise into a process.
