Where Janus is heading

Build the foundation. Then widen what it makes possible.

Janus OS is a long-term approach to helping people organize and direct AI-assisted work. This roadmap shows the direction we're pursuing, not guaranteed release dates or a promise that every idea will ship in its present form.

01
Governed foundations

Operating and being tested internally. Bounded tasks, defined checkpoints, review records and a development assembly line provide a first place to test whether work can move reliably while important decisions remain accountable. This is internal evidence, not production validation across customer environments.

02
Arbiter and workflow creation

In active development. Making these controls usable by people who understand their work but don't write software: clarify the intent, shape the steps, and manage exceptions through a useful human experience.

2.5
Coaching and learning from execution

Early-stage direction. Compare what was intended with what actually happened. Use evidence from mistakes, holds and variation to identify where a process needs to be clarified or improved. A finished coaching product is not yet claimed.

03
Toth and broader intelligence

Future exploration. Tools that can identify patterns across governed work, suggest changes and help people make better decisions without handing over final authority. Toth is a proposed part of that wider ecosystem, not a currently available product.

04
Connected organizational knowledge

Longer-term research direction. Helping an organization learn from useful work it has already done: connect relevant context, retrieve the right lessons, and surface relationships that otherwise disappear between projects. The particular database, query language or memory architecture remains open to testing.

The constant throughout

Better delegation should protect people's attention.

It should do that without surrendering their authority or understanding. We will keep testing the technology against that standard as Janus grows.
